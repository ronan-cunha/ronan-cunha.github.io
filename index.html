Welcome!

Professor · School of International Relations, Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV RI)

I am an economist and data scientist working on time-series econometrics, high-dimensional forecasting, machine learning, and geospatial computer vision. My research combines econometric modeling with deep learning and multi-sensor satellite imagery to study macroeconomic dynamics, environmental security, and illicit economies in Latin America.

I hold a PhD and an MSc in Economics from the São Paulo School of Economics (FGV EESP). At FGV, I am also a researcher at the Center for Quantitative Studies in Economics and Finance (CEQEF/FGV EAESP) and the Center for Applied Macroeconomics (CEMAP/FGV EESP), and I coordinate the Quantitative Methods Workshop (Oficina de Métodos Quantitativos) at FGV RI.
